About the Role
Newtonite is seeking a UI/UX Designer Intern to shape the visual identity and user experience of a
high-performance, AI-powered SaaS platform. This is a design-focused, hands-on role for students and fresh
graduates with strong foundations in interface design and visual storytelling, and a genuine eye for craft. This
is a design position, not a development one — no coding is required.
Beyond product interfaces, you will take on creative brand work, including conceiving an original mascot and
character system in the spirit of the characters that give products like Duolingo their personality. You will
work directly with the founding team, see your work shipped to a live product, and be evaluated for a full-time
role upon successful completion of the internship.
Key Responsibilities
UI/UX Design   Design intuitive, responsive interfaces for web and mobile — from user flows and wireframes
through to high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes in Figma. Every screen is expected to be clean,
considered, and built around the user’s goal.
Brand Character & Mascot Design   Conceive and design an original mascot and supporting character
system, including personality, expressions, poses, and illustration styles, that can live across the product
interface, marketing, and communication.
Design System   Build and maintain a scalable design system covering components, typography, colour,
iconography, and spacing, so that the product feels consistent and new work ships faster.
Research & Iteration   Run lightweight user research and usability testing, translate feedback into design
decisions, and iterate quickly and visibly.
Developer Handoff   Collaborate closely with engineers, delivering production-ready specifications, assets,
and clear design rationale so that what ships matches what was designed.
Skills & Requirements
Candidates should have working proficiency across most of the following:
Design Tools: Figma (essential); Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, or Procreate
Core UX/UI: Wireframing, prototyping, user flows, visual hierarchy, typography, colour theory, design
systems
Illustration & Character: Vector illustration, character design, expressive and emotive visual storytelling
Nice to Have: Motion design (After Effects, Lottie, or Rive), accessibility fundamentals (WCAG)
Portfolio: Required. A link (Behance, Dribbble, Figma, or personal site) showing both interface design and
illustration or character work

Candidate Profile
Creative Ownership   The ideal candidate approaches design problems with initiative — identifying gaps
proactively, proposing ideas independently, and following through to a polished result without close
supervision.
Design Rigour   A strong understanding of design as a discipline is essential. Candidates should be able to
explain the reasoning behind their choices in hierarchy, spacing, colour, and flow, not simply present
attractive screens.
Brand & Storytelling Sense   An instinct for personality and emotion in design. The ability to give a product
a memorable, consistent character that users connect with.
Communication   Clear, concise written and verbal communication is expected. The ability to present design
decisions, take feedback well, flag blockers promptly, and collaborate across a small remote team without
ambiguity is essential.
What We Offer
Production Exposure from Day One   This is not an observational or training-based internship. Selected
candidates will contribute to live product design from the outset, and their work will be seen by real users.
Foundational Creative Ownership   Rare for an internship: you will help define the visual identity and
character of a product from its early stages, building portfolio work that carries real weight.
Pre-Placement Offer   Newtonite is building a core team for the long term. Interns who demonstrate

Direct Access to Founding Team   Selected interns will work in close collaboration with the founders,
